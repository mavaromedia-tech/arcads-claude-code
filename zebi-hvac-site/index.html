Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

How often should my furnace and AC be serviced?

Once a year each: furnaces in early fall, air conditioners in spring. Annual tune-ups keep efficiency up, catch worn parts early, and are usually required to keep manufacturer warranties valid.

Repair or replace — how do I decide?

A good rule of thumb: if the unit is over 12–15 years old and the repair costs more than a third of a replacement, replacing usually wins on efficiency and reliability. We'll always give you both numbers and an honest recommendation.

Are you licensed for gas and refrigeration work?

Yes — Zebi Refrigeration & Air Conditioning Inc. is fully licensed, insured and T.S.S.A. registered. Gas-fired equipment and refrigerant handling are done by certified technicians, to code, every time.

Do you handle commercial refrigeration?

It's in our name for a reason. Walk-in coolers and freezers, display cases, prep tables and process refrigeration — installation, repair and preventative maintenance for restaurants, grocers and warehouses.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Mississauga and serve the surrounding GTA, including Brampton, Oakville, Etobicoke, Toronto, Milton, Burlington and Vaughan.
